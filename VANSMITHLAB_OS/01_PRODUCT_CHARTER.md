# Product Charter

## Definition

VANSMITHLAB is an independent, faceless digital encyclopedia of design and visual culture. It explains the systems, people, objects, techniques, images, ideas and histories that shape how the designed world looks and works.

Its scope includes fashion, costume, textiles, jewellery, photography, graphic design, typography, branding, product and furniture design, architecture, interiors, film/visual direction, material culture, technology and AI as they intersect with design.

It is a knowledge institution with an editorial surface — not a personal blog, trend feed, marketplace, moodboard, or fashion-news publication.

## Mission

Build a trusted, growing public reference for design: useful for a curious reader, reliable enough to begin research, and visually calm enough to invite long-form thought.

## Editorial identity

The default author is **VANSMITHLAB**, an institutional voice: precise, curious, neutral, source-aware and clear about uncertainty.

The Analysis section is the principal authorial surface when an argument matters more than neutrality — for example, “How religion has shaped fashionable dress.” Occasional signed essays may also be published there. They must:

- live in the `Analysis` or `Essays` content type, never masquerade as an encyclopedia entry;
- name the author and date;
- distinguish evidence, interpretation and opinion;
- retain citations and a corrections path;
- link to the neutral objects and glossary terms that underpin the argument.

Analysis releases follow `20_ANALYSIS_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`: a strong question, concrete hook, cultural conflict, sourced examples, an independent conclusion and a visual argument. They are not encyclopedia definitions with a more dramatic headline.

## Audience and jobs to be done

| Audience | Need |
| --- | --- |
| Curious reader | Understand a design term, person, object or movement without prior expertise. |
| Student / researcher | Find a reliable first map, sources, chronology and adjacent subjects. |
| Creative professional | Trace references, materials, techniques, visual language and lineage. |
| Collector / enthusiast | Navigate objects, people, collections and cultural context. |

## Product promise

Every visitor should be able to answer at least one of these questions quickly:

- What is this?
- Why does it matter?
- When and where did it emerge?
- Who or what is connected to it?
- What evidence supports this?
- Where can I explore next?

## Deliberate exclusions

- No unverified fashion-news feed.
- No anonymous opinion presented as fact.
- No generic AI image feed, trend board, or engagement bait.
- No closed black-box claims; important facts must be traceable.
- No personal-founder layer on ordinary encyclopedia pages.
- No full graph visualization, complex personalization or omnichannel social automation in the first public release.

## Success metrics

- A reader can reach a trusted answer in under three interactions from a search result or landing page.
- Every published factual page exposes verification, sources, last-reviewed date and relevant links.
- A changed Russian master safely updates its localizations and derivatives.
- Corrections are visible, versioned and propagated.
- The content graph grows in connected depth, not merely in article count.

## Reader question and editorial promise

For `analysis`, write the reader's concrete entry point before the abstract topic. Record the strong question, opening case/scene, one-sentence thesis, cultural conflict, author's independent angle and the reason this argument matters now.

## Required structure

For `analysis`, specify the planned hook, thesis, historical turn, image mechanics, cultural conflict, 3–5 sourced mini-analyses, current relevance, authorial conclusion and a visual package (hero plus at least 4 purposeful inline visuals; normally 5–8, including photographs/details only with a recorded source and rights basis). Map each visual to the example or claim it helps the reader inspect. Record any justified exception.

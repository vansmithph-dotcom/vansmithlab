# VANSMITHLAB — Analysis OS v2.0

Status: canonical editorial rule
Version: 2.0 / 2026-08-13

## Purpose

`Analysis` is the site's principal authorial format. It is not a shortened encyclopedia entry and not a neutral definition with a more dramatic title. Its formula is:

**авторский аргумент + культурная интрига + факты + позиция.**

The analysis should explain not only what a phenomenon is, but why it matters, where its conflict lies, what we usually overlook and how it changes fashion, image, design or culture. It should be entertaining and educational without sacrificing evidence.

## Division of work

ChatGPT may write the Russian draft and the English adaptation. VANSMITHLAB's editorial pass is responsible for checking, rewriting and approving the material: argument, readability, factual claims, sources, language, visuals, rights and RU/EN parity. The model that produced a draft may not approve its own factual output. A draft that fails the standard is rewritten, not published with a cosmetic label.

## The central question

Every analysis must answer one strong question:

**What does this theme explain about the contemporary visual world?**

“What is fashion film?” is a topic. “Why did fashion stop trusting the photograph and begin to think in motion?” is an analytical question. The question should create tension and lead to an arguable thesis, not to a dictionary definition.

## Dramaturgy

Use this sequence as the default editorial architecture:

1. **Hook.** Open with a scene, object, image, conflict, paradox, quotation, number or documented production moment in one or two strong paragraphs. Do not begin with a broad definition.
2. **Main thesis.** State one clear authorial thought that could be challenged. It must explain why the subject matters, not merely rename it.
3. **Historical turn.** Show where the phenomenon came from and why it emerged at that moment; keep background subordinate to the argument.
4. **Mechanics of the image.** Explain how the visual system works: frame, movement, light, body, sound, montage, interface, platform and viewer, selecting only what the subject needs.
5. **Cultural conflict.** Make the tension visible: art versus commerce, authorship versus algorithm, fashion versus technology, body versus screen, reality versus simulation, or elitism versus platform.
6. **Three to five key examples.** Write mini-analyses, not a list of names. For each example, give the concrete fact, describe what the reader should notice, explain its relation to the thesis and mark the author's interpretation.
7. **Why it matters now.** Connect the argument to AI, social platforms, brands, visual culture, the market or the viewer only where evidence supports the connection.
8. **Authorial VANSMITHLAB conclusion.** Return to the opening case, test the thesis and state the independent conclusion. Do not end with a neutral repetition of the introduction.

The default long-form shape is **8–12 substantial sections**, with developed paragraphs and transitions. Thirty short headings or a chain of generalized statements do not qualify as depth. A shorter structure requires a written exception in the brief.

## Tone and readability

The tone is alive, intelligent and slightly magazine-like, with an authorial intonation but without academic heaviness. Explain specialist terms at first use. Prefer concrete verbs and images to bureaucratic abstractions. The reader should feel: “I understand this phenomenon differently now.”

Weak: “Музыкальное видео является аудиовизуальным форматом, объединяющим звук и изображение.”

Preferred direction: “Клип сделал с песней то, чего уже не могла сделать обложка пластинки: дал ей тело, лицо, комнату, походку, цвет и миф.” The sentence still needs factual support where it makes a historical claim; vivid writing is not permission to invent.

## Fact and interpretation

Facts are the backbone, not filler between opinions. Each material factual claim requires a source or claim ID, a source tier and a clear boundary between what the evidence establishes and what the author infers. Use calibrated language where evidence is partial. Mark interpretation explicitly with `[CALLOUT type="interpretation"]`; use `critical-context`, `dispute` or `open-question` where useful. Never turn an uncited inference, brand mythology or press-release superlative into fact.

The brief and review must record:

- the central question and one-sentence thesis;
- the opening case or scene;
- three to five intended examples and why each matters;
- factual context, interpretation and opinion separately;
- the conflict the article investigates;
- the evidence and citations for every retained factual claim;
- the conclusion's return to the opening case.

## Visual argument

An analysis is a visual argument as well as a text. The hero is only the entry point. A full-length analysis must normally include **at least four purposeful inline visuals in addition to the hero**; the usual target is **5–8 inline visuals**. A shorter piece may use fewer only when the brief records a real editorial reason.

ChatGPT must attach the media research at drafting time. Each visual slot names a concrete source-page link (and an optional direct asset link), creator/rightsholder, title or subject, date/context, placement, supported example or claim, why it matters, suggested alt text/caption and a rights basis. This prevents a second blind search during layout while preserving the editorial gate: **the VANSMITHLAB editor makes the final choice** and verifies the links and rights, and may crop, replace or hold any image. A URL is a lead, not a licence.

The visual package should combine several kinds of evidence rather than repeat one image:

- documentary photographs, object details, frames or screenshots where they make a concrete example visible;
- an original mechanism diagram, annotated image or process map;
- a timeline, comparison board or visual contrast when the argument has a historical/systemic dimension;
- material, spatial or interface details that let the reader inspect the claim;
- an AI illustration or reconstruction only when it clarifies a verified mechanism and is visibly labelled as non-documentary.

The article should normally contain **3–5 concrete examples**, and the visual plan should show which image, diagram or comparison supports each example. A photograph is persuasive only when its subject, date/context, creator, source and rights basis are known; photography never replaces a citation. Captions must tell the reader what to notice and why it matters.

Each visual must explain something, not simply decorate the page. Every asset requires alt text, an informative caption, credit, provenance/rights state and AI disclosure where relevant. If rights are unclear, prefer an original diagram or a clearly disclosed AI illustration; it must not pretend to be documentary evidence.

## Rewriting existing dry materials

Old informational DOCX files are useful source material, not finished Analysis publications. Their facts, section map, media slots, source list and validation report may be retained as research inputs. The prose must be rewritten into the v2.0 dramaturgy. Do not preserve thirty dry chapters merely because they already exist.

## Editorial series roadmap

Build the section as a small number of connected authorial series rather than a queue of unrelated explanatory cards. The following editorial lenses are approved starting points; each individual article still requires its own brief, evidence review and visual package.

### Series 01 — Fashion as a system of belief

- `Как религия повлияла на мир моды` — the starting node; strengthen its conflict, examples and authorial ending where needed.
- `Почему мода похожа на религию` — shows, rituals, icons, prohibitions, seasonal calendars and the cult of designers.
- `Скромность как визуальная власть` — from monastic dress and uniform to luxury minimalism.
- `Почему чёрный цвет стал языком силы` — religion, mourning, discipline, avant-garde, Yohji Yamamoto, Rei Kawakubo and Rick Owens.

### Series 02 — The image is no longer only a photograph

- `Почему мода перестала доверять фотографии` — fashion film, movement, platform and screen.
- `Клип как фабрика мифа` — music video as the making of a visual identity, not only promotion.
- `Почему титры иногда важнее первой сцены` — Saul Bass, Kyle Cooper and the serial opening.
- `CGI: почему изображение больше не снимают, а собирают` — 3D scene, render, virtual light and AI.
- `Экран, который смотрит в ответ` and `Почему игры научили нас читать экран быстрее кино` — interface, HUD, map, inventory, aim and status.

### Series 03 — The brand as media

Explore the fashion house as a network of campaigns, shows, films, archives, ambassadors and platforms; possible entries include `Почему модный дом теперь похож на телеканал`, `Журнал больше не бумага`, `Почему Dazed и i-D важнее, чем просто молодёжные журналы`, `Visionaire: журнал, который отказался быть журналом` and `032c: как Берлин превратил журнал в культурную машину`.

### Series 04 — Material as ideology

Possible entries include `Почему бетон стал честным, хотя его все считали грубым`, `Мрамор: почему роскошь любит камень, который притворяется вечностью`, `Нейлон: как военный материал стал языком повседневной моды`, `Патина: почему старение стало эстетикой, а не дефектом` and `Стекло: почему прозрачность почти всегда обманчива`.

The first new priority after the existing religion-and-fashion node is **`Почему мода перестала доверять фотографии`**. It should connect fashion photography, fashion film, SHOWstudio, Nick Knight, NOWNESS, Miu Miu Women's Tales, short-form platforms, AI video and the shift from a single frame to a circulating stream. This is an editorial priority, not permission to publish an unresearched draft.

## Release gate

Hold an analysis for rewrite when the opening is abstract, the thesis is unclear, examples are only listed, paragraphs are thin, the authorial position is missing, the conclusion is neutral, the media is decorative or rights are unresolved, or RU/EN do not preserve the same argument and uncertainty. Release only after the standard evidence, rights, localization, accessibility and technical gates pass, with the decision recorded as `AUTO_APPROVE`, `AUTO_REVISE`, `NEEDS_USER_INPUT` or `DO_NOT_PUBLISH`.

The governing principle is:

**Энциклопедия объясняет, что это. Анализ объясняет, почему это изменило визуальную культуру.**

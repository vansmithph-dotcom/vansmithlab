\# Editorial Policy

Version: 1.0



\## Purpose



This document defines the editorial rules of VAN SMITH LAB.



These rules apply to every published knowledge object regardless of topic or content type.



\---



\## Editorial Principles



\- Accuracy before speed.

\- Verification before publication.

\- Neutrality before interpretation.

\- Transparency before certainty.

\- Consistency across all publications.



\---



\## Knowledge Objects



Every publication is a knowledge object.



Content types include:



\- Knowledge

\- Research

\- Visual Analysis

\- Case Study

\- Timeline

\- Gallery

\- Video

\- Editorial Opinion



All content follows the same editorial standards.



\---



\## Fact Verification



Every factual statement must be verified before publication whenever possible.



Facts are classified by verification status.



Verification status:



\- Verified

\- Multi-source Verified

\- Partially Verified

\- Unverified

\- Rumor

\- Retracted



No statement may be presented as verified without supporting evidence.



\---



\## Source Policy



Every publication must contain a source list.



Sources are ranked by reliability.



Priority:



1\. Official documents

2\. Primary sources

3\. Academic publications

4\. Museum and archive collections

5\. Professional industry publications

6\. Reputable media

7\. Community sources

8\. Anonymous sources



Anonymous or unverified sources cannot be used as the sole basis for factual claims.



\---



\## Rumors



Rumors are allowed only when they have editorial value.



Every rumor must:



\- be clearly labeled;

\- include its source;

\- include a disclaimer that it has not been independently verified.



Rumors must never be presented as facts.



\---



\## Opinions



Opinions are separated from factual content.



Editorial opinions:



\- are clearly identified;

\- are attributed to the author;

\- must not alter or replace verified facts.



\---



\## AI Content



Artificial intelligence may assist in:



\- research;

\- drafting;

\- summarization;

\- translation;

\- image generation;

\- video generation;

\- metadata generation.



AI-generated visual content that depicts reconstructed, hypothetical or illustrative scenes must be clearly identified.



AI-generated content remains subject to editorial verification before publication where required.



\---



\## Images



Images are classified as:



\- Original

\- Official

\- Licensed

\- Editorial

\- AI Illustration

\- AI Reconstruction



Image origin must always be identifiable.



\---



\## Videos



Videos follow the same verification standards as written content.



Every factual statement used in narration must satisfy the same editorial requirements.



\---



\## Updates



Knowledge objects are living documents.



New verified information may update existing publications.



Each update must:



\- receive a new version;

\- record the update date;

\- preserve update history.



\---



\## Corrections



Errors must be corrected promptly.



Corrections must not silently replace previous information.



Correction history remains publicly available.



\---



\## Related Knowledge



Every publication must include links to related knowledge objects whenever available.



Relationships are maintained across the entire knowledge library.



\---



\## Languages



Russian is the master editorial language.



English publications are editorial adaptations created after approval of the Russian version.



Both versions must preserve factual accuracy.



\---



\## Publication Requirements



A publication is eligible for release only when it contains:



\- title;

\- summary;

\- verified content;

\- verification status;

\- source list;

\- related knowledge objects;

\- last updated date;

\- version number.



\---



\## Editorial Responsibility



Editorial responsibility remains with VAN SMITH LAB.



Artificial intelligence assists the editorial process but is not considered an author.

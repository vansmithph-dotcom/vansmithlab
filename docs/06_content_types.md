\# Content Types

Version: 1.0



\## Purpose



This document defines all supported content types within VAN SMITH LAB.



Each content type is built from a knowledge object and follows the same editorial standards.



\---



\## Knowledge



Purpose:



Present verified factual information.



Characteristics:



\- Neutral

\- Structured

\- Continuously updated

\- Source-based



\---



\## Research



Purpose:



Analyze a subject in depth.



Characteristics:



\- Long-form

\- Evidence-based

\- May include editorial conclusions

\- References multiple knowledge objects



\---



\## Visual Analysis



Purpose:



Explain visual language through detailed analysis.



Examples:



\- Fashion collections

\- Campaigns

\- Product design

\- Architecture

\- Photography

\- Branding



\---



\## Case Study



Purpose:



Analyze a specific project or real-world example.



Structure:



\- Context

\- Process

\- Analysis

\- Results

\- References



\---



\## Timeline



Purpose:



Present events in chronological order.



Each event includes:



\- Date

\- Title

\- Description

\- Sources



\---



\## Gallery



Purpose:



Display curated visual materials.



Gallery may include:



\- Photographs

\- Illustrations

\- AI Images

\- Documents

\- Diagrams



Every media item contains metadata.



\---



\## Video



Purpose:



Present knowledge in video format.



Video includes:



\- Title

\- Description

\- Chapters

\- Transcript

\- References

\- Related Objects



\---



\## AI Workflow



Purpose:



Document AI production processes.



May include:



\- Workflow

\- Prompt

\- Parameters

\- Results

\- Limitations



\---



\## Prompt



Purpose:



Document reusable prompts.



Prompt page may include:



\- Purpose

\- Prompt

\- Variables

\- Compatible Models

\- Examples

\- Version History



\---



\## Comparison



Purpose:



Compare two or more knowledge objects.



Examples:



\- Brand vs Brand

\- Camera vs Camera

\- AI Model vs AI Model

\- Material vs Material



Comparisons use standardized attributes.



\---



\## Guide



Purpose:



Provide structured educational content.



Guides are instructional but remain fact-based.



\---



\## Reference



Purpose:



Provide quick factual lookup.



Examples:



\- Specifications

\- Definitions

\- Standards

\- Measurements

\- Terminology



\---



\## Glossary



Purpose:



Define terminology.



Each entry includes:



\- Definition

\- Related Terms

\- References



\---



\## Collection



Purpose:



Group related knowledge objects.



Examples:



\- Best Campaigns

\- Designers

\- AI Models

\- Materials

\- Brands



Collections do not duplicate original content.



\---



\## Editorial



Purpose:



Publish editorial opinion.



Editorial content:



\- identifies the author;

\- clearly distinguishes opinion from fact;

\- references supporting evidence where applicable.



\---



\## News Update



Purpose:



Record verified developments affecting existing knowledge objects.



News updates are temporary and may later be incorporated into the primary knowledge object.



\---



\## Common Requirements



Every content type includes:



\- Title

\- Summary

\- Knowledge Object ID

\- Version

\- Publication Date

\- Last Updated

\- Verification Status

\- Confidence Level

\- Source List

\- Related Objects



\---



\## Language



Russian is the primary editorial language.



English versions are editorial adaptations linked to the same knowledge object.

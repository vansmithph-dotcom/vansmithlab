\# VAN SMITH LAB Manifest

Version: 1.0



\## Mission



VAN SMITH LAB is an independent digital knowledge library dedicated to visual culture.



The project documents, verifies, structures and connects knowledge about fashion, luxury, photography, architecture, design, branding and artificial intelligence.



The objective is not to publish news, but to build a trusted, long-term knowledge archive.



\---



\## Core Philosophy



The Lab does not try to know everything.



It strives to clearly distinguish between:



• what is known;

• what is highly probable;

• what is uncertain;

• what remains unknown.



Trust is built not by claiming certainty, but by being transparent about the limits of knowledge.



\---



\## Principles



• Knowledge before opinion.

• Verification before publication.

• Sources before conclusions.

• Quality before quantity.

• Long-term value before trends.

• Transparency before certainty.

• Continuous improvement.

• Every statement has a confidence level.

• Every important fact should be traceable to its sources.



\---



\## Content



The Lab publishes:



\- Knowledge

\- Research

\- Visual Analysis

\- Case Studies

\- Timelines

\- Videos

\- Galleries

\- AI Workflows

\- Editorial Opinions



Every publication becomes part of the knowledge graph.



\---



\## Editorial Policy



Facts are separated from interpretations.



Interpretations are separated from opinions.



Opinions are clearly identified.



Rumors are never presented as facts.



AI-generated media is clearly identified whenever it represents reconstructed or illustrative content.



Every publication contains:



\- verification status

\- confidence level

\- update history

\- source list

\- related topics



\---



\## Knowledge Graph



Every object is connected with related objects.



Examples:



Brand ↔ Designer



Designer ↔ Collection



Collection ↔ Campaign



Campaign ↔ Photographer



Photographer ↔ Equipment



Material ↔ Product



Technique ↔ AI Workflow



Video ↔ Research



Research ↔ Knowledge



The library continuously grows by expanding and strengthening these relationships.



\---



\## Languages



Russian is the primary editorial language.



Every publication is created and verified in Russian first.



The English version is an editorial adaptation written for native English readers, preserving meaning rather than literal wording.



\---



\## Automation



The editorial workflow is AI-driven.



Artificial intelligence performs research, organization, drafting, media generation and publication.



Human review is applied only where editorial judgment or verification is required.



Every publication follows the same standardized production pipeline.



\---



\## Founder



VAN SMITH LAB is founded and curated by Ivan Melnik.



The project exists independently of its founder.



The founder appears only in research papers, editorials and opinion articles.



\---



\## Goal



To become the most trusted independent digital knowledge library dedicated to contemporary visual culture.

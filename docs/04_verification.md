\# Verification Policy

Version: 1.0



\## Purpose



This document defines the verification process for all knowledge objects published by VAN SMITH LAB.



Verification ensures that published information is accurate, traceable and transparent.



\---



\## Verification Principles



\- Every important factual statement should be verified whenever possible.

\- Verification is based on evidence, not authority.

\- Confidence is determined by available evidence.

\- Verification is an ongoing process.



\---



\## Verification Workflow



The standard verification process:



1\. Collect information

2\. Collect sources

3\. Evaluate source reliability

4\. Compare independent sources

5\. Identify conflicts

6\. Assign verification status

7\. Assign confidence level

8\. Publish

9\. Re-verify when new information becomes available



\---



\## Verification Status



Allowed values:



\- Verified

\- Multi-source Verified

\- Partially Verified

\- Unverified

\- Rumor

\- Retracted



\---



\## Confidence Levels



Allowed values:



\- High

\- Medium

\- Low

\- Unknown



Confidence reflects the strength of available evidence.



\---



\## Verification Criteria



A statement may be considered verified when:



\- it is supported by reliable sources;

\- no stronger contradictory evidence exists;

\- the supporting evidence is traceable.



\---



\## Conflicting Information



When sources disagree:



\- identify the conflicting statements;

\- document each position;

\- reference supporting sources;

\- do not resolve the conflict without sufficient evidence.



\---



\## Unverified Information



Unverified information:



\- must be clearly labeled;

\- must not be presented as established fact;

\- may be updated after additional verification.



\---



\## Rumors



Rumors require:



\- source identification;

\- clear labeling;

\- verification disclaimer.



Rumors are never treated as verified information.



\---



\## Retractions



Information must be marked as retracted when:



\- official corrections are published;

\- evidence proves previous information to be incorrect.



Retractions remain visible in version history.



\---



\## Re-verification



Knowledge objects should be re-verified when:



\- official information changes;

\- new primary sources become available;

\- significant corrections are published.



\---



\## Version History



Every verification update records:



\- Version

\- Date

\- Updated Sections

\- Verification Summary



Previous versions remain accessible.



\---



\## AI Assistance



AI may assist with:



\- source comparison;

\- inconsistency detection;

\- duplicate detection;

\- metadata generation.



Final verification follows the editorial rules of VAN SMITH LAB.



\---



\## Transparency



Every published knowledge object must display:



\- Verification Status

\- Confidence Level

\- Last Verification Date

\- Source List

\- Version Number



\---



\## Editorial Responsibility



Verification decisions are made according to this policy and remain the responsibility of VAN SMITH LAB.

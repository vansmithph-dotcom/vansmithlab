\# Automation

Version: 1.0



\## Purpose



This document defines the automation architecture of VAN SMITH LAB.



Automation standardizes the production, maintenance and distribution of knowledge objects.



\---



\## Objectives



The automation system shall:



\- reduce manual work;

\- maintain consistency;

\- improve scalability;

\- support continuous updates;

\- preserve editorial standards.



\---



\## Automation Scope



Automation may be used for:



\- Research

\- Source Collection

\- Metadata Generation

\- Classification

\- Translation

\- Media Generation

\- Internal Linking

\- Search Indexing

\- Publishing

\- Version Management

\- Monitoring



\---



\## Workflow



The standard automation workflow:



Research



↓



Source Collection



↓



Verification



↓



Knowledge Object Creation



↓



Metadata Generation



↓



Media Generation



↓



Internal Linking



↓



Publication



↓



Search Index Update



↓



Monitoring



↓



Revision



\---



\## Trigger Events



Automation may start when:



\- a new knowledge object is created;

\- an existing object is updated;

\- new sources become available;

\- scheduled verification occurs;

\- manual editorial review is requested.



\---



\## Metadata Automation



The system may automatically generate:



\- Slug

\- Summary

\- Keywords

\- Categories

\- Tags

\- SEO Metadata

\- Related Objects



Metadata remains editable.



\---



\## Relationship Automation



The system may automatically detect relationships between knowledge objects.



Suggested relationships require editorial verification before publication where applicable.



\---



\## Translation



Translation may be automated.



Russian remains the master version.



English is generated from the approved Russian version.



\---



\## Media



Automation may generate:



\- Illustrations

\- Diagrams

\- Infographics

\- Video Assets

\- Thumbnails



Generated media follows editorial policy.



\---



\## Publication



Publication automation may:



\- publish new objects;

\- publish updates;

\- rebuild indexes;

\- refresh related links;

\- update version history.



\---



\## Monitoring



The system may monitor:



\- official websites;

\- publications;

\- corrections;

\- new releases;

\- updated documentation.



Detected changes enter the verification workflow.



\---



\## Notifications



Automation may notify editors about:



\- new sources;

\- verification conflicts;

\- outdated information;

\- failed publication tasks;

\- pending reviews.



\---



\## Error Handling



Automation failures must:



\- be logged;

\- preserve existing published data;

\- allow manual recovery.



No published knowledge object may be deleted automatically.



\---



\## Version Control



Every automated update records:



\- Version

\- Date

\- Modified Fields

\- Update Summary



\---



\## Human Review



Human review may be required for:



\- conflicting information;

\- low-confidence verification;

\- editorial opinions;

\- legal or copyright issues.



\---



\## Consistency



All automated processes must comply with:



\- Manifest

\- Editorial Policy

\- Source Policy

\- Verification Policy

\- Knowledge Model



Automation supports the editorial process and does not replace editorial responsibility.

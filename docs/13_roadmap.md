The roadmap organizes project growth into sequential phases.

\## Success Criteria



The platform should provide:



\- Consistent editorial quality

\- Reliable verification

\- Structured knowledge

\- Strong internal linking

\- Scalable architecture

\- Efficient automation

\## Maintenance



The roadmap is reviewed periodically.



Completed phases remain documented.



Future phases are added without modifying completed milestones.



\---



\## Long-Term Vision



VAN SMITH LAB evolves into a continuously expanding digital knowledge library dedicated to contemporary visual culture.



Growth is driven by structured knowledge, editorial quality and standardized workflows.

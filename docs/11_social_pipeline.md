\# Social Pipeline

Version: 1.0



\## Purpose



This document defines how knowledge objects are adapted for social media while maintaining editorial consistency.



Social media extends the knowledge library and always links back to the original knowledge object.



\---



\## Principles



\- Knowledge first.

\- Accuracy before engagement.

\- One source of truth.

\- Platform-specific adaptation.

\- Consistent branding.



\---



\## Source



Every social publication originates from an existing knowledge object.



Social content never becomes the primary source of information.



\---



\## Supported Platforms



\- LinkedIn

\- Instagram

\- X

\- Threads

\- Pinterest

\- YouTube



Additional platforms may be added without changing the workflow.



\---



\## Workflow



Knowledge Object



↓



Platform Adaptation



↓



Media Selection



↓



Caption Generation



↓



Metadata Generation



↓



Publication



↓



Performance Monitoring



\---



\## Platform Adaptation



Each platform receives content adapted to its format.



Only presentation changes.



Facts remain identical.



\---



\## Media



Media may include:



\- Images

\- Carousels

\- Videos

\- Shorts

\- Reels

\- Infographics



All media references the same knowledge object.



\---



\## Caption



Captions should:



\- summarize the topic;

\- encourage exploration;

\- remain factually accurate;

\- avoid misleading statements.



\---



\## Hashtags



Hashtags should:



\- describe the topic;

\- improve discoverability;

\- remain relevant.



Hashtags must not alter meaning.



\---



\## Links



Whenever supported, publications should include a reference to the original knowledge object.



The website remains the canonical source.



\---



\## Scheduling



Publications may be scheduled automatically.



Scheduling does not modify content.



\---



\## Updates



When a knowledge object is updated:



\- related social content may be updated;

\- new publications may be generated;

\- previous publications remain archived where appropriate.



\---



\## Monitoring



The system may collect:



\- impressions;

\- engagement;

\- clicks;

\- shares;

\- saves.



Collected metrics do not influence factual content.



\---



\## Corrections



If a factual correction is published:



\- future social publications use corrected information;

\- existing publications may be updated or replaced when appropriate.



\---



\## Versioning



Generated social assets inherit the version of their originating knowledge object.



\---



\## Consistency



Every social publication follows:



\- Manifest

\- Editorial Policy

\- Source Policy

\- Verification Policy



Social media distributes knowledge but never replaces the knowledge library.

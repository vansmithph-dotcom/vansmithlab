## What Prada is

Prada is easier to understand not as a sequence of well-known objects but as a system. Within it the historical memory of the house, the choice of material, several brands, a cultural institution, a model of creative direction and corporate infrastructure all work simultaneously. This system was assembled gradually — from a Milan shop to an international group.

The starting point requires a careful formulation. The Prada Group's Annual Report 2017 indicates that in 1913 Mario Prada opened a shop in the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II in Milan.[^clm_c0e5dfd603536ac6] The same report describes his assortment as bags, travel trunks, beauty cases, accessories, jewellery and other luxury objects.[^clm_8314102067d2e536]

It matters that the founding logic of the house is already visible here: Prada did not begin with an abstract idea of "fashion" but with a specific place and a set of objects. The subsequent history shows how this foundation expanded — first into clothing, then into independent brands, art and a complex group.

## From shop to clothing

According to the Prada Group's Annual Report 2017, the first clothing collection was launched in Milan in 1988.[^clm_be45c8b81dd29d48] In the same corporate source Prada's debut in menswear is dated to 1993 and linked to the first men's clothing and footwear collection.[^clm_de54fae1c06c601a]

These two dates mark more than an addition to the assortment. They show the transition from a house of objects and accessories to a house able to build a coherent language of clothing for different directions. Prada's ready-to-wear should be seen as a change of scale of the whole system, not as a separate episode of the calendar.

## Nylon: material as method

The most telling part of this system is nylon. The Prada Group writes in its own historical material that Miuccia Prada's collection of nylon bags has existed since the 1980s, and roughly ten years later nylon entered the men's and women's ready-to-wear collections.[^clm_52e0c780ade772d0]

Museum records allow this history to be seen through specific objects. The Museum at FIT records a Prada nylon backpack of the early 1990s, produced in Italy, with the inventory number 2017.1.1.[^clm_edaa1256fff557dd] The Metropolitan Museum of Art holds a Prada dress of the autumn–winter 1995–1996 season, designed by Miuccia Prada; the description of the materials lists nylon, metal and Velcro.[^clm_83e0ee6aa5daa928]

Together these sources do not prove a legend of a single "moment of invention" and give no grounds for attributing an unconfirmed commercial effect to a specific model. They show something else: a material passes through the accessories and the clothing and becomes a repeatable way of linking the different parts of a fashion house.

## Two expansions of 1993

In 1993, according to the Annual Report 2017, the Miu Miu brand was established.[^clm_f8e2f67d26b7e156] The same report states that in 1993 Miuccia Prada and Patrizio Bertelli created Milano Prada Arte, subsequently Fondazione Prada, to develop their interests in the sphere of art and culture.[^clm_e86a5603f600a87c]

These events matter precisely as a pair. One widens the fashion structure through a new brand; the other creates a separate institutional frame for art and culture. Prada ceases to be only one fashion label: different but connected platforms of work form around the main house.

## A shared creative direction

In an official announcement the Prada Group stated that from 2 April 2020 Raf Simons would join Prada as co-creative director and work with Miuccia Prada with equal responsibility for creative input and decisions.[^clm_1f236f4edea0308a]

For the house's structure this is not merely a date of personnel. The wording "equal responsibility" describes a model in which the creative direction is built as a joint work rather than as a one-sided transfer of authorship. This is why this episode belongs to the arrangement of Prada, not only to the biography of two designers.

## Versace and the contemporary scale of the group

On 2 December 2025 Prada S.p.A. completed the acquisition of Versace from Capri Holdings.[^clm_8943ac45720ef9ac] The Prada S.p.A. communication on the closing of the deal states that the necessary regulatory approvals had been obtained by its completion.[^clm_9fec59eb5f44821c]

As of 31 December 2025 the Prada Group Company Profile listed within the group Prada, Miu Miu, Church's, Car Shoe, Versace, Pasticceria Marchesi and Luna Rossa.[^clm_8dd499fd463cfd06] As of the same date the group indicated 25 owned factories,[^clm_ae202078f26c5b2d] 17,901 employees including Versace,[^clm_c79cbc8057ab2d68] and 843 directly operated stores.[^clm_6ac15db1c1c61da3]

The financial snapshot helps grasp the scale but does not replace the history of design. For the 2025 financial year Prada Group's net revenue was €5.718 billion; the results communication notes 9% growth year on year and 8% organic growth.[^clm_fca9304af866fd57] The same communication gives Miu Miu's growth as 35% year on year at constant currency,[^clm_2ba1135d58475232] and the contribution of Versace to net revenue from the date of closing as €65 million.[^clm_784c51c121889338]

## Why Prada is a system

Prada's history joins several different logics. The 1913 shop gives the house an origin and a memory of place. Clothing widens the world of objects into a full fashion language. Nylon links accessories and ready-to-wear through a repeatable material method. Miu Miu and Fondazione Prada develop the system in two directions — fashion and culture. The shared creative direction establishes a way of taking authorial decisions, and the contemporary Prada Group provides the production, retail and corporate scale.

Prada's influence cannot therefore be reduced to one bag, one material or one designer. The objects become clearer when the infrastructure is visible that allows them to appear, to pass between categories and to preserve a link to the house's history.

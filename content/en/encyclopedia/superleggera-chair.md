# Superleggera

*Minimum mass as a design problem: craft and industry*

## Editorial thesis

Superleggera matters to VANSMITHLAB not because it is called an icon, but because one object reveals the chain author → material → production → space → image → canon. Minimum mass as a design problem: craft and industry.

## Reader question

Why did Superleggera become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because Superleggera makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

The 699 Superleggera is a wooden chair by Gio Ponti, produced by Cassina since 1957. Its premise is clear: reduce mass and section until lightness becomes a measurable engineering quality rather than a visual gesture.

## 2. Origins and prehistory

Ponti drew on the tradition of the lightweight Ligurian Chiavari chair and refined it with Cassina over several years. Superleggera emerged through progressive structural reduction and the translation of a craft archetype into controlled industrial production.

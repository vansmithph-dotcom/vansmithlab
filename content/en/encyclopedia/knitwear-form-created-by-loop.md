# Knitwear — Form Created by the Loop

*An elastic structure in which material and construction emerge at the same time.*

> Knitwear differs from woven clothing because its surface is built from a system of interlinked loops. This structure provides stretch, the ability to follow the body and the possibility of shaping not only by cutting but through the formation of the fabric itself. Knitwear dissolves the hard boundary between material and form: the surface can also be the structure. This lets it move easily between underwear, sport, everyday clothing and high fashion.

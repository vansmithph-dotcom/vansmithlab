# Wiggle Side Chair

*Cardboard as structural material and a critique of familiar value*

## Editorial thesis

Wiggle Side Chair matters to VANSMITHLAB not because it is called an icon, but because one object reveals the chain author → material → production → space → image → canon. Cardboard as structural material and a critique of familiar value.

## Reader question

Why did Wiggle Side Chair become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because Wiggle Side Chair makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

The Wiggle Side Chair is Frank Gehry’s 1972 Easy Edges chair built from laminated corrugated cardboard with hard edge panels. Its undulating profile turns packaging material into load-bearing structure and an expressive furniture silhouette.

## 2. Origins and prehistory

Gehry experimented with ordinary cardboard and found that laminating layers with controlled corrugation direction produced unexpected strength. Easy Edges translated this effect into objects that were materially cheap yet formally complex.

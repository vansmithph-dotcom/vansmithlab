# Lining — the Invisible Architecture of Clothing

*The inner layer that controls friction, construction cleanliness, movement and garment life.*

It is an ethics of the invisible: the wearer may rarely see it in full, yet continually experiences it through movement and use.

A lining is patterned with its own movement allowance and attached so that it does not pull the outer shell.

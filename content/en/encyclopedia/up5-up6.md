# UP5/UP6

## Editorial thesis

UP5/UP6 matters to VANSMITHLAB not because it is called an icon, but because one object reveals the chain author → material → production → space → image → canon. Body, politics, polyurethane foam and radical design.

## Reader question

Why did UP5/UP6 become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because UP5/UP6 makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

UP5/UP6 is an armchair and tethered spherical ottoman from Gaetano Pesce’s 1969 UP series. The anthropomorphic chair uses moulded polyurethane foam and stretch upholstery, while the ball on a cord turns the set into a figurative image of body and constraint.

## 2. Origins and prehistory

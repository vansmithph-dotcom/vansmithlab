# Mezzadro

*A found industrial element transformed into a domestic object*

## Editorial thesis

Mezzadro matters to VANSMITHLAB not because it is called an icon, but because one object reveals the chain author → material → production → space → image → canon. A found industrial element transformed into a domestic object.

## Reader question

Why did Mezzadro become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because Mezzadro makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

Mezzadro is a stool by Achille and Pier Giacomo Castiglioni, designed in 1957 and put into production by Zanotta in 1970. Its metal seat derives from an agricultural tractor and is joined to a springing steel support and wooden base.

## 2. Origins and prehistory

The Castiglioni brothers investigated ready-made industrial components as construction material for new objects. The project transferred an agricultural machine element into the interior without disguising its origin.

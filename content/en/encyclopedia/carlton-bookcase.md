# Carlton

*Memphis: furniture as sign, colour and refusal of neutrality*

## Editorial thesis

Carlton matters to VANSMITHLAB not because it is called an icon, but because one object reveals the chain author → material → production → space → image → canon. Memphis: furniture as sign, colour and refusal of neutrality.

## Reader question

Why did Carlton become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because Carlton makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

Carlton is a bookcase and room divider designed by Ettore Sottsass in 1981 for Memphis. Laminated planes, stepped diagonals and vivid graphics make it furniture, sign and almost flat architecture at the same time.

## 2. Origins and prehistory

## 16. Professional practice

For professional designers, Carlton is useful as a methodological case. Carlton attacks the idea that functional furniture should look quiet. It stores and divides space, but it also demands attention as a character — part totem, part façade, part pictogram.

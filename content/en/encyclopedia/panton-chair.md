# Panton Chair

*One material, continuous form and a new plastics industry*

## Editorial thesis

Panton Chair matters to VANSMITHLAB not because it is called an icon, but because one object reveals the chain author → material → production → space → image → canon. One material, continuous form and a new plastics industry.

## Reader question

Why did Panton Chair become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because Panton Chair makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

The Panton Chair is an S-profile cantilever chair developed by Verner Panton and brought to serial production with Vitra. Its historical importance lies in making a complete chair as a continuous plastic shell without a conventional frame or separate legs.

## Editorial thesis

## Reader question

Why did 606 Universal Shelving System become important to design history if it is only one piece of furniture?

## Short answer

Because 606 Universal Shelving System makes a new relationship between form, body, material and production visible. Its historical role lies in the design problem it formulated and the way that formulation outlived its original moment.

## 1. Definition and boundaries of the object

The 606 Universal Shelving System is a modular storage system designed by Dieter Rams in 1960 for Vitsœ. Instead of a finished cabinet, the user receives infrastructure: tracks, shelves, cabinets, desks and fittings that can be assembled, rearranged and extended.

## 2. Origins and prehistory

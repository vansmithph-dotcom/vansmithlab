# The magazine as system

*The publication as a machine of selection, rhythm, image, and authority.*

## About this collection

A magazine is rarely analysed as a production system, though it is the system that explains its influence: who selects, at what rhythm it appears, how a spread is built, and why the cover costs more than everything else. This route assembles the mechanics of publishing.

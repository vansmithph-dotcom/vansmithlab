# Design Language

*How visual systems become languages — and how to read them*

## What is design language

A design language is not a style or a set of devices. It is a system of decisions: how form relates to function, how hierarchy is built, how visual elements address a viewer. This collection covers movements, principles and authors whose work shaped a shared professional vocabulary.

## Movements and principles

- [Bauhaus](/en/encyclopedia/bauhaus-art-production-system) — the first attempt to unite art, craft and industrial production into a single system.
- [Modernism](/en/encyclopedia/modernism-form-follows-function) — "form follows function" as a professional ethic.
- [Constructivism](/en/encyclopedia/constructivism-engineering-agitation-visual-discipline) — engineering, agitation and visual discipline.
- [Swiss Style](/en/encyclopedia/swiss-style-grid-typography-objective-information) — grid, typography, objective information.
- [De Stijl](/en/encyclopedia/de-stijl-grid-primary-colors-universal-order) — grid, primary colours, universal order.
- [Postmodernism](/en/encyclopedia/postmodernism-quotation-irony-return-of-sign) — quotation, irony, the return of the sign.
- [Memphis](/en/encyclopedia/memphis-color-play-antifunctional-energy) — colour, play, antifunctional energy.
- [Minimalism](/en/encyclopedia/minimalism-reduction-space-discipline-of-form) — reduction, space, discipline of form.
- [Art Deco](/en/encyclopedia/art-deco-luxury-geometry-machine) — luxury, geometry, machine.
- [Art Nouveau](/en/encyclopedia/art-nouveau-jugendstil-line-nature-total-interior) — line, nature, total interior.
- [Brutalism](/en/encyclopedia/brutalism-mass-concrete-structural-honesty) — mass, concrete, structural honesty.
- [Deconstructivism](/en/encyclopedia/deconstructivism-fracture-tension-unstable-form) — fracture, tension, unstable form.

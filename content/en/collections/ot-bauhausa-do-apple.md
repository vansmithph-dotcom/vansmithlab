# From Bauhaus to Apple

*How school, object, and interface turned out to be one discipline: a line from the teaching workshop to the screen environment.*

## About this collection

This route follows one line: how a method worked out in a teaching workshop reached the industrial object and then the screen interface. At every step the material changes and the question does not — how to record a decision so that another person can execute it.

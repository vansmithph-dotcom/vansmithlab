# Design Systems

*How organisational structures turn a method into a repeatable practice*

## About the collection

A design system is not a toolkit, a component library or a style guide. It is a way of organising decisions that lets a method work without the author's constant presence. This collection covers organisations, authors and movements that turned method into system.

# Design Systems

*How organisational structures turn a method into repeatable practice*

## About the collection

A design system is not a toolkit, a component library or a guideline. It is a way of organising decisions that allows a method to function without the constant presence of its author. This collection covers organisations, authors and movements that turned a method into a system.

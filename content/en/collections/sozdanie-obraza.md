# Image-Making

*Photography, editorial, production: how visual images are made*

## About the collection

An image is not a snapshot. It is the result of decisions: light, frame, chemistry, model, editing, publication context. This collection covers photographic processes, authors and publications that shaped the language of the image in fashion, documentary and fine-art photography.

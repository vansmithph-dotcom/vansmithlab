# How a chair explains the twentieth century

*The chair as a laboratory of body, material, ideology, and production: one object on which every technology of the century was tested.*

## About this collection

The chair is the most convenient object for a history of design: small enough to be remade often, complex enough for every new technology to be tested on it. This route reads the century through a sequence of constructional decisions.

# Transparency: glass, interface, photography

*Transparency as a promise of access and simultaneously as a technique of visual control.*

## About this collection

Transparency is almost always presented as openness: the mechanism is visible, the process is visible, there is nothing to hide. This route tests the promise from the other side — transparency is just as often a way of governing exactly what one is permitted to see.

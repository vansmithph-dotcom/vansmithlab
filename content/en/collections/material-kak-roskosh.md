# Material as luxury

*Luxury arises not from the price of raw material but from processing, edition, and a regime of explanation.*

## About this collection

Historically luxury meant a rare substance. This route shows how value moved first into precision of working and then into the system around the object — warranty, service, document, and institutional ratification.

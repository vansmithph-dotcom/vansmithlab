# Japan: from Metabolism to teamLab

*Japanese visual culture between megastructure, emptiness, media, and bodily experience.*

## About this collection

The route joins two lines usually treated separately: the engineering utopia of growth and the discipline of emptiness. Both proceed from one question — how to design under conditions of limited space and constant renewal.

## Megastructure and growth

- [Metabolism](/en/encyclopedia/metabolism-city-living-organism-architecture-of-growth) — the city as living organism and an architecture of growth.
- [SANAA](/en/articles/sanaa) — lightness, transparency, and the dissolution of volume.
- [Tadao Ando](/en/glossary/architects/tadao-ando) — concrete, light, and the discipline of empty space.

## The discipline of emptiness

- [Minimalism](/en/encyclopedia/minimalism-reduction-space-discipline-of-form) — reduction, space, and the discipline of form.
- [MUJI: the absence of brand as a brand project](/en/articles/muji-no-brand-as-brand-project) — the absence of branding as a consistent position.
- [Charred Wood](/en/encyclopedia/charred-wood-yakisugi-protection-black-texture) — charred wood: protection and black texture.

## Media and bodily experience

- [teamLab](/en/articles/teamlab) — an image the viewer walks into.
- [Motion Design — Graphic Design Gains Time](/en/encyclopedia/motion-design-graphic-design-gains-time) — graphic design that gained time.
- [The Exhibition — Temporary Architecture of an Argument](/en/encyclopedia/exhibition-temporary-architecture-of-argument) — the exhibition as temporary architecture of argument.

# The store as media

*Retail space produces the brand image before any purchase happens.*

## About this collection

When the transaction moved into an interface, the physical point lost its monopoly on the deal and acquired a different task: to explain, to show material at close range, and to produce images. This route reads retail as a publishing channel.

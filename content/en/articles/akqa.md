## 1. Origins and context

AKQA was founded in London in 1994. Its founders include Ajaz Ahmed and James Hilton. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. AKQA entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: treats digital space not as a distribution channel but as a place where a brand can become a service, product or interactive system. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. Nike Training Club: first anchor example

Nike Training Club. Digital training products showed how sports-brand communication can move from messaging toward a useful tool. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

Research and experimental projects. The company regularly uses computation, data and new interfaces to design behaviour rather than image alone. ## 5. A system rather than a single device

With AKQA, it is more useful to look for a system of choices than one repeated visual gesture. Treats digital space not as a distribution channel but as a place where a brand can become a service, product or interactive system. Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. ## 6. Influence on the discipline

Became a reference point in the shift from digital advertising toward designing digital experiences and services. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Multidisciplinarity can easily become a vague promise to do everything. The useful test is whether different disciplines genuinely combine inside one solution. This matters for AKQA because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

AKQA positions itself as an international design and innovation company.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

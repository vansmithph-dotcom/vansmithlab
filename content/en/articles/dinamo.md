## 1. Origins and context

Dinamo was founded in Berlin in 2013. Its founders include Johannes Breyer and Fabian Harb. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Dinamo entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: connects type design with software, research, publishing and unconventional distribution models. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. ABC Favorit: first anchor example

ABC Favorit. The family became an example of a direct contemporary sans system retaining character through proportion and detail across broad use. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

## 4. Font Gauntlet: second anchor example

Font Gauntlet. Tools and experiments show the studio treating type not only as letterform but as a technical object with behaviour. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. It is therefore evidence of a working system rather than an addition to a list of famous projects.[^clm_case2]

## 5. A system rather than a single device

With Dinamo, it is more useful to look for a system of choices than one repeated visual gesture. Connects type design with software, research, publishing and unconventional distribution models. Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. VANSMITHLAB records authorship through mechanism rather than superficial resemblance.

## 6. Influence on the discipline

Represents a generation of foundries for which code, licensing and digital distribution are part of design itself. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Type lives not in an alphabet specimen but in text, interfaces, wayfinding and multiple languages. Expression must be tested alongside readability, character support, technology and licensing. This matters for Dinamo because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

Dinamo continues as an independent type practice with teams across several cities.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

## 1. Origins and context

TBWA Worldwide was founded in Paris in 1970. Its founders include William Tragos, Claude Bonnange, Uli Wiesendanger and Paolo Ajroldi. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. TBWA Worldwide entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: builds work by identifying category conventions and deliberately breaking them through its Disruption methodology. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

Showed that a large network can build coherence through a shared strategic process rather than one visual style. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. ## 7. Critical distance

Advertising influence cannot be measured by campaign fame alone. Cultural force should be separated from commercial success, awards and media scale. This matters for TBWA Worldwide because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

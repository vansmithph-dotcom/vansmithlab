## 1. Origins and context

MoMA — Architecture & Design was founded in New York in 1932. Its founders include The Museum of Modern Art. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. MoMA — Architecture & Design entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: collects architecture and design as interconnected fields, shaping history through exhibitions, acquisitions, archives and research programmes. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 7. Critical distance

A museum does not only preserve history; it produces it through acquisitions, exhibitions and interpretation. Collections reflect curatorial choices, omissions and changing definitions of design. This matters for MoMA — Architecture & Design because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

The department continues exhibition, research and collection work, with 2026 programming including new projects around information, ecology and contemporary design.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

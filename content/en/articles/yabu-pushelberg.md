## 1. Origins and context

Yabu Pushelberg was founded in Toronto in 1980. Its founders include George Yabu and Glenn Pushelberg. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Yabu Pushelberg entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: builds interiors as complete hospitality worlds connecting architecture, furniture, lighting, art and the behaviour of staff and guests. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. Park Hyatt New York: first anchor example

Park Hyatt New York. The hotel interior expresses luxury through sequences of material, light and scale rather than demonstrative decoration. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

## 4. Edition hotels: second anchor example

Edition hotels. Work with the hotel brand explores how a common identity can adapt to different cities without repeating one interior literally. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. It is therefore evidence of a working system rather than an addition to a list of famous projects.[^clm_case2]

## 5. A system rather than a single device

With Yabu Pushelberg, it is more useful to look for a system of choices than one repeated visual gesture. Builds interiors as complete hospitality worlds connecting architecture, furniture, lighting, art and the behaviour of staff and guests. Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. VANSMITHLAB records authorship through mechanism rather than superficial resemblance.

## 6. Influence on the discipline

Had major influence on contemporary international luxury hospitality and the connection between interiors and product design. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Interior design can be reduced to photogenic surfaces. The profile therefore considers planning, circulation, light, material longevity and the relationship between space and use. This matters for Yabu Pushelberg because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

Yabu Pushelberg continues from Toronto and New York across spaces, products, lighting and brands.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

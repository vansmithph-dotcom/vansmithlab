## 1. Origins and context

Actual Source was founded in Utah in 2015. Its founders include Davis Ngarupe. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Actual Source entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: combines graphic-design studio, publishing, retail and product making, blurring the line between commissioned identity and its own editorial culture. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 5. A system rather than a single device

With Actual Source, it is more useful to look for a system of choices than one repeated visual gesture. Combines graphic-design studio, publishing, retail and product making, blurring the line between commissioned identity and its own editorial culture. Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. VANSMITHLAB records authorship through mechanism rather than superficial resemblance.

## 6. Influence on the discipline

Represents a generation of studios in which self-directed retail and publishing become part of design research. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

A publisher is not a neutral channel. Author selection, format, price, print run, design and distribution shape the canon as much as text and imagery do. This matters for Actual Source because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

Actual Source continues to operate as a studio and publishing platform in the United States.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

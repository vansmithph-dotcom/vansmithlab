## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: repeatedly changed its own model with technology, moving from screen graphics and film production to networked products, services and organisational transformation. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

This matters for R/GA because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology.

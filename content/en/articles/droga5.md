## 1. Origins and context

Droga5 was founded in New York in 2006. Its founders include David Droga. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Droga5 entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: combines strategy, cultural observation and strong narrative devices, often turning an advertising problem into an event or fictional story. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. Dundee: first anchor example

Dundee. The Tourism Australia campaign disguised advertising as a continuation of a familiar film story and used the reveal itself as a media event. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

## 4. Decode Jay-Z: second anchor example

Decode Jay-Z. The Bing project distributed pages of Jay-Z’s book across real urban surfaces, linking search, outdoor media and audience participation. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. It is therefore evidence of a working system rather than an addition to a list of famous projects.[^clm_case2]

Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. ## 6. Influence on the discipline

Helped establish an agency model in which an idea can exist simultaneously as campaign, environment, action and digital service. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Advertising influence cannot be measured by campaign fame alone. Cultural force should be separated from commercial success, awards and media scale. This matters for Droga5 because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

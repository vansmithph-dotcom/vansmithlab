## 1. Origins and context

Studiopepe was founded in Milan in 2006. Its founders include Arianna Lelli Mami and Chiara Di Pinto. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Studiopepe entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: connects interiors, objects, exhibition staging and colour research, creating spaces as complete authored statements. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. Club Unseen: first anchor example

Club Unseen. The temporary club during Milan design week used a hidden venue as a laboratory for furniture, light, material and social scenario. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

## 4. The Intimacy: second anchor example

The Intimacy. The 2026 project continues to investigate domestic space as an emotional and tactile environment rather than a collection of isolated objects. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. It is therefore evidence of a working system rather than an addition to a list of famous projects.[^clm_case2]

## 5. A system rather than a single device

With Studiopepe, it is more useful to look for a system of choices than one repeated visual gesture. Connects interiors, objects, exhibition staging and colour research, creating spaces as complete authored statements. Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. VANSMITHLAB records authorship through mechanism rather than superficial resemblance.

## 6. Influence on the discipline

Represents a Milanese tradition in which an interior studio also acts as an author of objects, exhibitions and visual research. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Interior design can be reduced to photogenic surfaces. The profile therefore considers planning, circulation, light, material longevity and the relationship between space and use. This matters for Studiopepe because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

Studiopepe continues its independent Milan practice across interiors, objects and exhibitions.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

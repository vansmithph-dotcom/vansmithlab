## 1. Origins and context

LAYER was founded in London in 2015. Its founders include Benjamin Hubert. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. LAYER entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: combines strategy, industrial design, engineering and visual identity with particular attention to new technologies and user behaviour. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. Ledger Stax: first anchor example

Ledger Stax. The hardware wallet explores how a complex digital function can be expressed through a simple physical object, screen and everyday gesture. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

## 4. Bang & Olufsen Beosound Emerge: second anchor example

Bang & Olufsen Beosound Emerge. The speaker adopts the proportions of a book so a technical object can sit naturally on a domestic shelf and rethink speaker typology. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. It is therefore evidence of a working system rather than an addition to a list of famous projects.[^clm_case2]

## 5. A system rather than a single device

With LAYER, it is more useful to look for a system of choices than one repeated visual gesture. Combines strategy, industrial design, engineering and visual identity with particular attention to new technologies and user behaviour. Similarity between projects may therefore lie in research procedure, team organisation, material attitude or sequence of work rather than identical form. VANSMITHLAB records authorship through mechanism rather than superficial resemblance.

## 6. Influence on the discipline

Represents a contemporary studio where digital technology and physical objects are designed as one user experience. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

A product cannot be judged from a prototype photograph alone. Its design is revealed by manufacturing, cost, repair, longevity, ergonomics and how it ages in use. This matters for LAYER because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

LAYER works from London with international companies across technology, furniture and consumer products.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

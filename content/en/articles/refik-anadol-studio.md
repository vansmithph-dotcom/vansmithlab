## 1. Origins and context

Refik Anadol Studio was founded in Los Angeles in 2014. Its founders include Refik Anadol and Efsun Erkılıç. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Refik Anadol Studio entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: uses large datasets, machine learning and architectural surfaces to create moving abstract environments in which computation becomes visible form. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. WDCH Dreams: first anchor example

WDCH Dreams. Projection on Walt Disney Concert Hall transformed institutional archives into changing imagery linked to the building envelope. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

Unsupervised. The MoMA project used the museum collection as material for machine learning and made computational interpretation part of a public exhibition. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. Made machine-learning generation and data a prominent direction in large-scale public media art. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Immersion can conceal a weak idea. Technology becomes artistic material when it changes perception of time, body, space or information rather than simply increasing spectacle. This matters for Refik Anadol Studio because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

The studio is developing Dataland and other projects at the intersection of art, data, architecture and artificial intelligence.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

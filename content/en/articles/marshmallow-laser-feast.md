## 1. Origins and context

Marshmallow Laser Feast was founded in London in 2012. Its founders include Robin McNicholas, Barney Steel and Ersin Han Ersin. The organisation emerged within a professional context in which tools, markets and cultural expectations were changing the definition of the discipline itself. Marshmallow Laser Feast entered that process not as an isolated author but as a collective structure capable of turning a method into repeatable practice.[^clm_origin]

## 2. How the method works

The core principle can be stated simply: uses virtual reality, spatial sound and scientific data to translate invisible natural processes into bodily experience. This matters because method defines not an exterior style but a way of making decisions: what to research, which constraints matter, where conflict lies and how form connects to use. The repeatability of that approach is what makes institutional influence visible.[^clm_method]

## 3. In the Eyes of the Animal: first anchor example

In the Eyes of the Animal. The work shifts the human viewpoint by imagining forest perception through other species and questioning the usual centre of observation. The example matters as observable evidence of method: the relationship between problem and form can be inspected in a specific object, image, space or system rather than accepted from studio rhetoric.[^clm_case1]

## 4. We Live in an Ocean of Air: second anchor example

We Live in an Ocean of Air. The installation makes breath, metabolism and the relation between people and trees visible through moving spatial visualisation. The second example tests whether the approach remains coherent when scale, client, medium and cultural context change. It is therefore evidence of a working system rather than an addition to a list of famous projects.[^clm_case2]

## 6. Influence on the discipline

Helped shape a form of sensory scientific storytelling in which data becomes spatial experience rather than diagram alone. Influence becomes visible when questions and working methods enter the practice of other authors, schools, clients or institutions. This does not necessarily mean direct imitation; more often the range of acceptable solutions and the professional vocabulary itself changes.[^clm_impact]

## 7. Critical distance

Immersion can conceal a weak idea. Technology becomes artistic material when it changes perception of time, body, space or information rather than simply increasing spectacle. This matters for Marshmallow Laser Feast because reputation can turn successful decisions into universal mythology. VANSMITHLAB separates documented fact, interpretation and editorial judgment: influence is recognised without replacing analysis of limits, context and collective authorship.

## 8. Why the organisation matters now

Marshmallow Laser Feast continues to create artworks about nature, perception and interconnected living systems.[^clm_current] The relevance of the profile is not defined only by current commissions. The practice remains a useful comparison point because it reveals which professional questions survive changing technologies and fashions and which belonged to a specific historical moment.

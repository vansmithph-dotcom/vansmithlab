# Mats Gustafson

*Watercolour, silhouette and silence: fashion illustration as reduction*

> Mats Gustafson builds fashion imagery through reduction: watercolour wash, line, silhouette and empty field replace detailed description. His work shows that illustration can be both precise and indeterminate, capturing the character of a collection without becoming a technical drawing.

## Editorial thesis

Mats Gustafson builds fashion imagery through reduction: watercolour wash, line, silhouette and empty field replace detailed description. His work shows that illustration can be both precise and indeterminate, capturing the character of a collection without becoming a technical drawing.

## Reader question

Why can a few transparent washes sometimes convey a collection more precisely than a detailed rendering of every element?

## Short answer

Swedish artist and illustrator Mats Gustafson has worked between fashion and art since the 1970s. Watercolour, pastel and graphite let him reduce the figure to rhythm and mass, while his long collaboration with Dior shows how drawing can accompany a contemporary fashion house without replacing photography.

## 1. Sweden and an early visual culture

Gustafson was born in Sweden in 1951 and entered professional illustration in the 1970s, gradually connecting editorial work with independent drawing. [^clm_vsl_ill_005_001]

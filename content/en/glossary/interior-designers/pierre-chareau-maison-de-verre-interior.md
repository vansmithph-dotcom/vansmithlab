# Pierre Chareau

*Interior as mechanism: furniture, glass, movement and the architecture of Maison de Verre*

## Editorial thesis

Pierre Chareau matters because furniture, partition, light and architecture cease to be separate categories in his work. In the Maison de Verre and elsewhere, the interior becomes a mechanism of behaviour: panels move, light is filtered, furniture changes function and industrial details enter domestic space without disguise.

## Reader question

How did an interior designer turn a house into a complex machine of privacy, movement and light without losing human scale?

## Short answer

Chareau worked in furniture and interiors in France during the first half of the twentieth century. His major work, the Maison de Verre, made with Bernard Bijvoet for the Dalsace family, combined glass block, metal structure, moving elements and purpose-designed furniture into one spatial system.

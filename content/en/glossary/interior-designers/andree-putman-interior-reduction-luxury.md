# Andrée Putman

*Restraint, graphic rhythm and a new luxury of public interiors*

> Andrée Putman changed the idea of the luxury interior by rejecting compulsory expensive decoration. Black-and-white graphics, precise materials, light, historical design and an ability to work across hotels, shops and aircraft allowed restraint to become a form of status in its own right.

## Editorial thesis

Andrée Putman changed the idea of the luxury interior by rejecting compulsory expensive decoration. Black-and-white graphics, precise materials, light, historical design and an ability to work across hotels, shops and aircraft allowed restraint to become a form of status in its own right.

## Reader question

How can an interior feel luxurious without displaying wealth through decorative excess?

## Short answer

Putman came to interior design through music, fashion and cultural work. Her company Écart International brought forgotten modernist objects back into production, while hotels and shops of the 1980s and 1990s showed that luxury could be built from proportion, contrast and intellectual selection.

## 1. A path through music, fashion and culture

Putman did not begin as a conventional interior architect. Musical training and work across cultural and fashion environments shaped an interdisciplinary perspective. [^clm_vsl_int_004_001]

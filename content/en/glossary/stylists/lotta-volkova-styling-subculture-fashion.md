# Lotta Volkova

*The stylist as cultural montage: subculture, runway and a new system of taste*

## Editorial thesis

Lotta Volkova matters not as the author of one “bad taste” look but as a stylist who made incompatibility into a working method. Sportswear, Soviet and post-Soviet associations, luxury fashion, club culture and deliberate awkwardness can coexist when held together by a clear cultural position.

## Reader question

How does a stylist turn things once considered unfashionable or incompatible into a new language of high fashion?

## Short answer

Volkova grew up in Vladivostok, studied in London and became a visible author of the new European fashion of the 2010s. Work with Gosha Rubchinskiy, Vetements, Balenciaga and later Miu Miu showed the stylist as someone who connects collection, casting, show and campaign into one cultural montage.

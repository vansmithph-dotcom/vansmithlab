# Andrew Bolton

*Fashion exhibition as argument: body, object, history and the mass museum audience*

> Andrew Bolton changed the scale of museum fashion exhibitions by turning them from displays of clothes into complex cultural arguments. His projects at The Metropolitan Museum of Art’s Costume Institute connect object, body, scenography, art history, technology and mass media attention, which also raises the critical question of where scholarship ends and spectacle begins.

## Editorial thesis

Andrew Bolton changed the scale of museum fashion exhibitions by turning them from displays of clothes into complex cultural arguments. His projects at The Metropolitan Museum of Art’s Costume Institute connect object, body, scenography, art history, technology and mass media attention, which also raises the critical question of where scholarship ends and spectacle begins.

## Reader question

How can a curator bring millions of visitors to clothing without turning the museum into a luxury display window?

## Short answer

Bolton joined The Metropolitan Museum of Art in 2002 after working at the Victoria and Albert Museum. Exhibitions including Alexander McQueen: Savage Beauty, China: Through the Looking Glass, Manus x Machina, Heavenly Bodies and the 2026 project Costume Art show how fashion can be discussed through religion, technology, body, national imaginaries and art history.

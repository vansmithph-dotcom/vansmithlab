# Tonne Goodman

*Fashion editing between image, clothing and environmental responsibility*

> Tonne Goodman matters as an editor who connected the classical Vogue tradition with a later question: how fashion is made, used and discarded. Her career maps a shift from producing desirable images toward responsibility for the system that sustains them.

## Editorial thesis

Tonne Goodman matters as an editor who connected the classical Vogue tradition with a later question: how fashion is made, used and discarded. Her career maps a shift from producing desirable images toward responsibility for the system that sustains them.

## Reader question

Can a fashion editor who spent decades producing desire use the same authority to change a culture of consumption?

## Short answer

Goodman moved from work around Diana Vreeland and Calvin Klein to a long career at Vogue. Later she focused on sustainability, showing that editorial work can include not only selecting an image but also the lifespan of a garment, reuse and brand responsibility.

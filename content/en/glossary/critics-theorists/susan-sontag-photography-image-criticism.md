# Susan Sontag

## Editorial thesis

Susan Sontag matters to visual culture because she moved the discussion of photography from technique and taste into ethics, power and spectator behaviour. Her writing asks not only what an image shows but what happens to a person who endlessly looks at, collects, publishes and consumes other people’s reality.

## Reader question

Why can photography, which appears to be direct evidence of reality, simultaneously create distance, habit and power over what is pictured?

## Short answer

Sontag was a writer, essayist, director and public intellectual. The 1977 collection On Photography and the 2003 book Regarding the Pain of Others became key points in debates about photography, war, spectator distance and whether images of suffering can change behaviour.

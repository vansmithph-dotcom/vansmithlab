# Beatriz Colomina

*Architecture as media: photography, publication, health and the public production of space*

## Editorial thesis

Beatriz Colomina changed architectural theory by showing that modern buildings are not produced only on construction sites. Photography, magazines, advertising, exhibitions and publication participate in producing architecture by shaping its audience, authorial image and the meaning of private and public.

## Reader question

Can a photograph of a building be part of the architecture itself when most people will never see the original and will know it only through publications?

## Short answer

Colomina is an architectural historian and theorist long based at Princeton University. Privacy and Publicity connected modern architecture to mass media; later research examined X-rays, health, radical pedagogy and relationships among bodies, technologies and the built environment.

## 1. Barcelona and architectural education

Colomina received architectural training in Barcelona before developing an international academic research career. [^clm_vsl_ct_005_001]

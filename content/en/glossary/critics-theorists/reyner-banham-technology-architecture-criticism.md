# Reyner Banham

## Editorial thesis

Reyner Banham expanded architectural criticism by forcing it to look not only at facades and composition but also at air conditioning, cars, domestic technology, advertising imagery and urban infrastructure. His writing shows that modern environments are shaped by technical systems and popular culture as much as by canonical buildings.

## Reader question

What happens to architectural history if air conditioning, freeways and domestic appliances are treated as seriously as monumental buildings?

## Short answer

Banham began with engineering training and became one of the most influential British architectural critics of the second half of the twentieth century. Theory and Design in the First Machine Age, The Architecture of the Well-Tempered Environment and Los Angeles: The Architecture of Four Ecologies made technology, climate and everyday infrastructure legitimate subjects of architectural theory.

Before academic architectural history Banham received technical training and worked in the aircraft industry. [^clm_vsl_ct_004_001]

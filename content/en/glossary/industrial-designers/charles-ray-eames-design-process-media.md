# Charles & Ray Eames

*A collaborative practice as laboratory: furniture, architecture, film, exhibitions and learning through play*

> Charles and Ray Eames matter not as a collection of isolated icons but as a way of working. They connected material experiment, industrial production, architecture, photography, film, exhibitions and toys in one research practice in which ideas were repeatedly tested through prototypes and use.

## Editorial thesis

Charles and Ray Eames matter not as a collection of isolated icons but as a way of working. They connected material experiment, industrial production, architecture, photography, film, exhibitions and toys in one research practice in which ideas were repeatedly tested through prototypes and use.

## Reader question

How did a husband-and-wife studio turn different forms of design and media into one system for investigating everyday life?

## Short answer

The Eames Office, established in Los Angeles in 1941, worked simultaneously across furniture, architecture, film, graphics, exhibitions and educational objects. Instead of separating disciplines, Charles and Ray built a continuous process: observation, model, test, production, feedback and another version.

# Ettore Sottsass

*From Olivetti to Memphis: the object as carrier of emotion, sign and cultural conflict*

## Editorial thesis

Ettore Sottsass matters because he repeatedly refused to treat the industrial object as a neutral machine of function. From Olivetti equipment to radical furniture and Memphis, he explored how colour, material, symbol and emotional awkwardness could change the relationship between people and things.

## Reader question

How did a designer with deep knowledge of industrial production become one of the major critics of cold functionalism?

## Short answer

Sottsass moved through architecture, electronics, furniture, ceramics, graphics and writing. His practice was not a straight path toward Memphis; experience in industrial design gave him the knowledge needed to recognise the limits of modernist rationality and to treat objects as emotional and cultural signs.

Sottsass was born in 1917 and trained in architecture in Turin, entering the profession through architecture before rapidly expanding into objects, exhibitions and graphics. [^clm_vsl_ind_005_001]

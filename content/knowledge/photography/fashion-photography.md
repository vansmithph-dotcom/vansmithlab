Fashion photography is a visual discipline combining storytelling,
styling, lighting, composition and art direction to communicate
fashion concepts through imagery.

## Purpose

The goal of fashion photography is not simply to document clothing,
but to create emotion, identity and aspiration.

## Core Elements

- Composition
- Lighting
- Styling
- Color
- Mood
- Narrative
